The 5-Minute Rule

When resistance is high on your Level 1 subject, commit to just 5 minutes of focused work. 80% of procrastination breaks once momentum is generated.

📝

Closed-Book Rule

Never re-read notes highlighting passively. Solve problems or blurt with notes shut. True retention occurs during the struggle of retrieval.

⚡

Interleaved Practice

Do not study one subject for 6 hours straight. Rotate subjects between blocks to train your brain to differentiate problem categories under exam pressure.
